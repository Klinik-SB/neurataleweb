## Prompt GENESIS

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: main image for GENESIS, a research platform for sociological multi-agent simulation, on a premium light-themed AI company website.
Create a refined editorial 3D illustration, landscape 3:2. Show a small society of distinct abstract humanoid agents, 14 to 18 simple elegant figures of frosted glass and ceramic, interacting in three clusters on a softly sculpted isometric environment. The environment has subtle connected terrain terraces representing coast, grassland and mountains, interpreted as a sophisticated research model rather than a videogame. Fine teal, blue and muted amber luminous paths link agents within and between groups, conveying dialogue, relationships and emergence. Give a few agents different poses or directions, and connect groups with one or two bridging agents.
Art direction: luminous white studio, pale blue-gray background, teal and sage accents, a little warm amber, realistic materials, soft shadows, crisp crafted forms, calm expensive scientific editorial aesthetic, strong central composition filling the frame. No text, logos, numbers, screens, UI or charts. No large brain, no fantasy city, no robots with faces, no claim of consciousness. This is conceptual artwork, not a screenshot or photograph of a real facility. One polished image.

## Prompt Perturbaciones radiobiológicas

Use case: scientific-educational.
Asset type: conceptual main image for an experimental radiobiology research project about changing the order of radiation fractions while keeping the same total dose.
Create a refined scientific editorial 3D illustration, landscape 3:2, on a luminous white and pale peach studio background. Two elegant transparent cell-culture dishes sit side by side on a light laboratory surface. Each dish contains equally rich, detailed translucent cell clusters with delicate membranes and nuclei, seen as a beautiful magnified scientific representation. Behind and above each dish, show a slim floating ribbon of exactly five stylized luminous pulse blocks: the first ribbon has the order tall teal, short amber, medium blue, tall teal, short amber; the second has the same five block types in a different order. The identical collection of blocks conveys equal totals and different sequence. The two cell cultures should look equally healthy and equally abundant, without implying an established difference in outcome.
Art direction: precise glass materials, soft macro depth, subtle peach and turquoise light, clean elegant composition, realistic scientific model aesthetic, carefully controlled highlights. Clearly conceptual illustration; not real microscopy. No actual irradiation apparatus, no laser beams striking cells, no text, labels, numbers, DNA helix, charts, logos, UI or watermarks. One polished image.
