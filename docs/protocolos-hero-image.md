# Imagen principal del Buscador de protocolos

Archivo final: `dist/assets/art/protocolos-investigacion-criterios.png` (1536 × 1024).

Ilustración conceptual creada con ImageGen integrado, sin CLI, a pedido del usuario. Representa búsqueda documental y revisión de criterios con estados y datos pendientes. No es una captura de la aplicación ni representa una admisión a un ensayo clínico.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: main website image for a clinical research protocol search and documentary preselection tool, part of Neuratale's light-themed medical software website.
Create a refined editorial 3D illustration in landscape 3:2. On a luminous white and pale blue studio surface, arrange three elegant translucent glass document cards and a compact visual comparison matrix. The document cards have abstract clean blue-gray lines and small neutral icons, with tabs and subtle molecule motifs suggesting research protocols. A beautifully crafted turquoise optical magnifier hovers over one card, revealing a few precise highlighted criteria. Nearby, a tidy grid of small teal, blue and amber tiles represents criteria with different review states and missing information, connected to the document cards with delicate lines. Include a couple of unfilled outlined tiles to convey pending data. The composition should communicate finding relevant studies and reviewing criteria with documentation, without implying that a patient has been accepted into a trial.
Premium scientific editorial aesthetic, realistic frosted glass and ceramic materials, soft shadows, controlled turquoise and warm amber accents, crisp details, strong balanced composition that fills the frame. No people, patient records, DNA helix, medical treatment apparatus, text, numbers, logos, UI screenshots, fake certificates or watermarks. One polished conceptual image.
